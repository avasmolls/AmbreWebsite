# AMBRÉ
A fake fragrance company with a focus on warm, gourmand, luxury scents. I want to website to give exactly that.
This is a solo project I am taking on to focus on my personal skills in front-end and back-end development and experimenting with AI to create a brand I must make a website for. 
I also played around with AI to create mockups for me to view, as if it was a client giving me ideas of the what they would want their website to look like.
